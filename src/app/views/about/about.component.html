History

A brief history of how it begun

Domysuma was founded in September 2017 and incorporated in 2019 in Kitale, Kenya, with a mission to bridge the gap between the construction industry and youth employment.

Starting with government institution projects, the company diversified into private buildings, commercial structures, road works, and electrical services.

To address Kenya's building collapse crisis, Domysuma Architects LTD was launched to provide economical, sustainable designs and construction consultancy based on on-site experience.
